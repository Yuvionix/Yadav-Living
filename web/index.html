Our story

Rooted in Deoghar.
Built around people.

Yadav Living brings community living and Yadav Living PG together to serve different chapters of life: a residential community and a place for students to stay. We pair a personal, owner-led approach with simple digital tools for the everyday details.

For residents, that means a clearer way to follow society services and updates. For students, it means a direct path to registration, room options and owner coordination. Behind both is the same idea: take care of the place, and the people who make it home.
